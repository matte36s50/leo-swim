Where these times come from

Every time comes from USA Swimming's official times database. The lap splits come from the UVAC meet result sheets. The Bronze, Silver and AG cutoffs are the boys 11–12 cutoffs printed on the 2026 meet results. "Old Leo" is his earliest time in that event, and "yards ahead" is how far old Leo would still have to swim when today's Leo finishes, if both swam at an even pace.
